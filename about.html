About M Scouting

A desktop software product for individuals and businesses.

M Scouting is desktop software designed to help individuals and businesses organize prospect information, manage customer records and streamline prospecting workflows.

Our purpose

Prospecting is easier when information is organized. M Scouting gives people a dedicated desktop tool to keep that information in order, so more time goes to the work that matters.

Our vision

To be a simple, dependable tool for people and teams who do prospecting work every day.
