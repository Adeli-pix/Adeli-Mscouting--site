Frequently asked questions

Quick answers.

What is M Scouting?

M Scouting is desktop software that helps individuals and businesses organize prospect information, manage customer records and streamline prospecting workflows.

Is M Scouting desktop software?

Yes. It is an application you install and use on your computer. This website only presents the product.

Who can use it?

Individuals and businesses who do prospecting work.

How do subscriptions work?

You choose an Individual or Business plan, billed monthly or annually. Access to the software follows your subscription or trial. See the pricing page for current prices.

What is a business seat?

A seat is one person using M Scouting under a business plan. Business plans start at 10 seats, and the price depends on the number of seats.

How can customers contact support?

Use the contact page. Support details are listed there.
